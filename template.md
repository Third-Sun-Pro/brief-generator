Technology and Accessibility 

To ensure a smooth launch we’ll need details about your domain, hosting, email marketing, analytics (if any), file storage, and related services. We’ll send you a short Technical Information Questionnaire to gather these specifics.

A NOTE ABOUT ACCESSIBILITY: Our goal is to design a site that is modern, user-friendly, and accessible to all. Meeting current accessibility standards (like WCAG 2.1 AA) may require modifications to colors, font sizes, or typefaces— which may differ slightly from your existing brand materials. Our team will work with you to strike the right balance between your current visual identity and legal/ethical requirements, ensuring your site is as welcoming as possible to every visitor.

If you have any questions on the technology and accessibility parts of the project, please let us know at any time.

Mobile-First Considerations

As website owners, you likely spend more time viewing your site on desktop than your typical visitor—over 60% of web traffic comes from mobile devices. We work with you to take your desktop vision and turn it into a site with responsive layouts, touch-optimized interfaces, and performance optimization. Our goal: balance your design ideas with the practical needs of your mobile audience.
